2027 → 2028 High School Rule Change

The car got longer.
The floor got much heavier.

TSA's High School Dragster Design event reverses course for 2027-2028: after 2026 pushed toward shorter, lighter cars, the new guide grows the body and raises the mass floor sharply — 60g minimum in 2027, 85g in 2028. Judging changed too: a formal Documentation Portfolio replaces the old loose drawing + materials list, and GenAI use is now explicitly permitted, with a required AI-use reflection. This tool checks your design against both years and simulates the 20m run under a physics model you can calibrate against your own timed runs.

What actually changed

Body length, min2026: 250mm2027→28: 265→290mm
Body length, max2026: 260mm2027→28: 275→300mm
Body mass, min2026: 50g2027→28: 60→85g
Documentationdrawing + list→ full portfolio
GenAI toolsbanned→ permitted

Source: TSA High School Competitive Events Guide for the 2027 and 2028 National Conferences, Dragster Design event (Rule DB, official specification table). This is a genuine reversal of the 2025→2026 shrink-the-car trend — 2027-2028 cars are bigger and meaningfully heavier at the floor, which changes launch dynamics.

Method & sources

Rule numbers are pulled directly from the official TSA event guide. The physics model is a standard launch-and-coast approximation — it is not an official TSA tool and doesn't know your bearings, your wood, or your track surface. Calibrate the thrust and friction sliders against a stopwatch time from your own car for a result that means something.

Not affiliated with TSA. This is an independent engineering aid built from the publicly posted High School Competitive Events Guide. Always check the current-year Themes & Problems page at tsaweb.org for special annual design challenges before finalizing a build — the spec table above covers dimensional/mass rules only. It does not cover the Documentation Portfolio requirements (title page, technical drawing, materials list, and the required Resources & AI Reflection form) or the semifinal scoring rubric — check the official rating form for those.
Primary source: TSA High School Competitive Events Guide for the 2027 and 2028 National TSA Conferences — Dragster Design event (Rule DB/A/S/P/E/W, Regulations & Requirements, Official Rating Form). This guide also formally permits GenAI use for this event (with a required Resources and AI Reflection form) — a reversal from the outright GenAI ban in the 2025–2026 guide.
